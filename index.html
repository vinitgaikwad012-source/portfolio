FreightO

A freight marketplace that connects shippers with truck drivers, and keeps both sides protected.

Shippers post a load, a carrier accepts, and payment is released only when the proof of delivery is uploaded. The fair-trip price is worked out from distance, fuel price, mileage and driver rate, so nobody has to haggle blind.

Built with KYC checks, escrow-style payouts and OTP-confirmed delivery, in a calm black, white and navy look that businesses trust.
